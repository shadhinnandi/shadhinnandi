
# 🧠 Practice Problem Bank

Welcome to the final challenge. This section is designed to be your training ground for technical interviews, competitive programming, and university exams. The problems here are a step up from the chapter exercises. They require you to combine multiple concepts and think critically about efficiency.

## 🎯 The Goal

The purpose of this problem bank is to develop:
*   **Pattern Recognition:** The ability to see a new problem and relate it to a problem you've solved before.
*   **Logic Under Pressure:** Solving problems that aren't straightforward, similar to an interview setting.
*   **Optimization Mindset:** Thinking not just about a correct solution, but a *good* solution.

The problems are inspired by the logic found on platforms like LeetCode (Easy/Medium) and Codeforces (800-1500 rating), but are adapted to be solvable with the C concepts you've learned in this course.

---

## 📂 File Structure

*   [**Questions**](./questions.md): This section contains **only the problem statements and hints**. You should always start here. Try to solve the problems on your own for at least 30-60 minutes before looking at the solution.
*   [**Solutions**](./solutions.md): This section contains the **fully explained solutions** to all problems. Use this to check your work or to understand a new approach if you get stuck.
