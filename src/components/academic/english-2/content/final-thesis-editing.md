# Thesis Statement Editing

## The task

The final paper's question 2A gives pairs of statements and asks you to label each one:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | An **announcement** rather than a thesis statement |
| **TN** | **Too narrow** to be developed in an essay |
| **TB** | **Too broad** to be developed in an essay |
| **OK** | A statement that **makes a clear point** |

No slide is dedicated to this task, so this lesson uses the thesis statements in the final essay slides as the reference for what "OK" looks like, and the definitions in the question itself for the other labels.

## The four kinds of statement

### A: Announcement

An announcement tells the reader **what the writer is going to do**, not **what the writer thinks**. It names the topic but makes no point.

**Signals:** *In this essay…*, *This essay will discuss…*, *I am going to write about…*, *This article is about…*, *The subject of this paper is…*, *…will be discussed.*

| Announcement | Revised to OK |
|---|---|
| In this essay, I will talk about social media. | Social media helps students share information, build networks and learn new skills. |
| The effects of fast food will be discussed. | Fast food harms health by causing weight gain, raising blood pressure and replacing nutritious meals. |

### TN: Too narrow

A too-narrow statement is a **single fact or tiny detail**. There is nothing to develop: once you have said it, the essay is over.

**Signals:** a specific number, date, price or single event; a statement that can be checked in one second.

| Too narrow | Revised to OK |
|---|---|
| My phone's battery lasts twelve hours. | Smartphones have changed how students study, communicate and manage their time. |
| The bus fare from Mirpur to Badda is forty taka. | Public transport in Dhaka is cheap but crowded, slow and unsafe for many passengers. |

### TB: Too broad

A too-broad statement covers a subject **far too large** for a five-paragraph essay. A whole book could not cover it.

**Signals:** *everything, everywhere, all, the world, history, life, society, technology, people* used without limits; huge topics with no focus.

| Too broad | Revised to OK |
|---|---|
| Sports are important all over the world. | Playing team sports helps university students stay fit, manage stress and build friendships. |
| Technology has changed human life. | Online banking has made payments faster, safer and more convenient for small businesses. |

### OK: A clear point

A good thesis is **specific enough to cover in an essay, general enough to need several paragraphs, and it makes a clear point**. The theses in the final slides are all OK:

- *A rainy day can be described through its beautiful surroundings, soothing sounds, and the different activities of people during the rain.*
- *Offline and online classes differ significantly in terms of interaction, flexibility, and learning environment.*

An OK thesis may or may not list its three points. The exam labels a statement OK if it *makes a clear point* that an essay can develop.

## How to decide quickly

Ask three questions in order:

1. **Does it announce?** (*In this essay…*, *…is about…*, *…will be discussed*) → **A**
2. **Is it just one fact or a tiny detail?** → **TN**
3. **Is the subject huge, with words like *everything*, *the world*, *life*?** → **TB**
4. Otherwise, does it make a clear, developable point? → **OK**

### The pairs strategy

The exam gives the statements in **pairs**. Usually one of each pair is OK and the other is A, TN or TB. Find the OK statement first, then decide what is wrong with its partner. This halves the difficulty.

## Revising a weak thesis

| Problem | How to fix it |
|---|---|
| Announcement | Delete *In this essay I will…* and state your actual point about the topic |
| Too narrow | Ask *why does this fact matter?* and widen it to a point with reasons |
| Too broad | Narrow the **who** (students, farmers), **where** (Dhaka, villages), **what** (one aspect) |
| Vague | Replace *good, bad, important* with specific reasons |

### Before and after

| Before | Problem | After |
|---|---|---|
| This essay is about climate change. | A | Climate change threatens Bangladesh through rising sea levels, stronger cyclones and saltwater in farmland. |
| Climate change affects the whole planet. | TB | Rising sea levels are forcing coastal families in Bangladesh to leave their homes and land. |
| The sea level rose by three millimetres last year. | TN | Rising sea levels are damaging farmland, drinking water and homes in coastal Bangladesh. |

## Revision summary

> **A** announces (*In this essay…*). **TN** is one fact. **TB** is a huge subject. **OK** makes a clear point an essay can develop.
> In each pair, find the OK statement first.

## Practice

:::practice Practice 1 · Basic | Label the statements
Write **A**, **TN**, **TB** or **OK** beside each statement.

i. This essay will describe my hometown.
ii. Education is important for everyone in the world.
iii. Regular reading improves students' vocabulary, concentration and imagination.
iv. My university was founded in 2003.
v. Online shopping saves time, offers more choice and often costs less than traditional shopping.
vi. Music has existed throughout human history.
--- skill
Question 2A: classifying thesis statements
--- answer
i. **A** (announces what the essay will do).
ii. **TB** (education, for everyone, in the world: far too large).
iii. **OK** (clear point with three developable reasons).
iv. **TN** (one fact; nothing to develop).
v. **OK** (clear point with a plan).
vi. **TB** (all of human history).
:::

:::practice Practice 2 · Exam format | Labelled pairs
Write A beside the announcement, TN beside the statement too narrow to be developed in an essay, TB beside the statement too broad, and OK beside the statement that makes a clear point.

1. a. Food is necessary for life.
   b. Eating a healthy breakfast helps students concentrate, stay energetic and avoid unhealthy snacking.
2. a. In this paper, the problems of traffic in Dhaka are going to be explained.
   b. Traffic jams in Dhaka waste time, increase pollution and raise transport costs.
3. a. Volunteering builds young people's empathy, leadership and communication skills.
   b. I volunteered at a blood donation camp last Friday.
4. a. Mobile banking has made payments easier for people in rural areas.
   b. Money has always been important in every society.
--- skill
Applying the pairs strategy
--- hint
In each pair, find the OK statement first. Then decide whether its partner announces, is a single fact or covers too much.
--- answer
1. a. **TB** · b. **OK**
2. a. **A** · b. **OK**
3. a. **OK** · b. **TN**
4. a. **OK** · b. **TB**
:::

:::practice Practice 3 · Guided writing | Revise the weak statements
Rewrite each statement as an OK thesis.

i. This essay is about smartphones. *(A)*
ii. The internet has changed the world. *(TB)*
iii. Our campus has two cafeterias. *(TN)*
--- skill
Revising a weak thesis into a clear, developable one
--- answer
i. Smartphones help university students study more efficiently through online resources, digital notes and quick communication with classmates.

ii. Online learning platforms have made higher education more accessible, flexible and affordable for students in Bangladesh.

iii. A good campus cafeteria improves student life by providing affordable meals, a place to relax and opportunities to meet friends.
