# Descriptive Essay

## Definition and purpose

A descriptive essay **paints a picture with words**. It describes a person, place, object, event or experience so vividly that the reader can see, hear and feel it. Like the descriptive paragraph from the midterm, it uses **sensory details** and a **dominant impression**, but it develops three aspects of the subject in three full paragraphs.

| Essay type | Main question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Descriptive** | *What is it like?* |
| Cause and effect | *Why does it happen? What does it lead to?* |
| Argumentative | *What should we believe or do, and why?* |
| Compare and contrast | *How are two things alike or different?* |

A descriptive essay does **not** argue, explain causes or tell a long story. If you find yourself writing "firstly, I believe…", you have drifted into argument.

## Structure and outline template

The slides' model, *A Rainy Day*, shows the structure.

| Paragraph | Purpose | In *A Rainy Day* |
|---|---|---|
| **Introduction** | Hook, general statement, background, thesis naming three aspects | Hook: *The sky suddenly turns dark…* Thesis: *…through its beautiful surroundings, soothing sounds, and the different activities of people* |
| **Body 1** | First aspect, with sensory detail | Surroundings: dark clouds, greener trees, shiny roads, smell of wet soil |
| **Body 2** | Second aspect | Sounds: tapping raindrops, tin roofs, rustling leaves, distant thunder |
| **Body 3** | Third aspect | People: tea indoors, students with umbrellas, rickshaw pullers, children in puddles |
| **Conclusion** | Restate the impression, summarise, final thought | *…not merely a change in weather but a beautiful experience filled with freshness, peace, and life* |

### Choosing the three aspects

The aspects must be **different angles** on the subject, not three examples of the same thing. Useful sets:

| Subject | Possible aspects |
|---|---|
| A place | appearance · sounds and atmosphere · people and activities |
| A person | appearance · personality · actions or influence |
| An event or festival | preparations / decorations · activities · food, music and feelings |
| A season or time | sights · sounds and smells · how people live in it |

The slides order the aspects from what you **see** to what you **hear** to what **people do**: from the scene to the life within it.

## Writing procedure

1. **Decide the dominant impression** (*peaceful*, *chaotic*, *joyful*, *inspiring*).
2. **Choose three aspects** that are different angles on the subject.
3. **Write the thesis:** *[Subject] can be described through its [aspect 1], [aspect 2] and [aspect 3].* (The slides' pattern; vary it once you are confident.)
4. **Collect sensory details** for each aspect: sight, sound, smell, touch, taste.
5. **Complete the outline** in the slides' format.
6. **Write the introduction:** a vivid hook (a scene works best), a general statement, two or three sentences of background, then the thesis.
7. **Write each body paragraph:** topic sentence, then six to eight detailed sentences moving in a clear order (near to far, morning to evening, outside to inside), then a closing sentence.
8. **Write the conclusion:** restate the impression in new words, recall the three aspects, end with a reflection.
9. **Check tense:** present for a typical scene or place; past for a particular visit or memory. Keep it consistent.

## Model essays

Three complete models: a place visited, a festival and a person. Each follows the slides' outline. The copy button copies the essay only.

:::model Model essay 1 · A place | A Visit to Cox's Bazar Sea Beach
--- outline
- **Topic:** A Visit to Cox's Bazar Sea Beach
- **Thesis Statement:** My visit to Cox's Bazar can be described through the vast beauty of the sea, the lively atmosphere of the beach and the unforgettable sunset.
- **Supporting Points/Plan of Development:** i) The vast beauty of the sea ii) The lively beach atmosphere iii) The unforgettable sunset
- **Topic sentence of the first supporting paragraph:** The first sight of the sea at Cox's Bazar is breathtaking in its size and beauty.
- **Topic sentence of the second supporting paragraph:** The beach itself is full of life, colour and sound throughout the day.
- **Topic sentence of the third supporting paragraph:** The most memorable part of the visit was the sunset over the Bay of Bengal.
- **Concluding sentence:** Cox's Bazar is not just a famous tourist spot but a place where nature reminds us how small and lucky we are.
--- answer Essay
The bus turned a corner, and suddenly a wide silver line of water appeared on the horizon. Cox's Bazar, on the south-eastern coast of Bangladesh, is famous for having one of the longest natural sea beaches in the world. Every year, thousands of visitors travel there to escape the noise of the cities and enjoy the open sea air. Last winter, I visited the beach with my family for the first time, and the experience still feels fresh in my memory. My visit to Cox's Bazar can be described through the vast beauty of the sea, the lively atmosphere of the beach and the unforgettable sunset.

The first sight of the sea at Cox's Bazar is breathtaking in its size and beauty. The water stretched endlessly in front of me until it met the pale blue sky. Long white waves rolled towards the shore one after another, broke into foam and slid back over the smooth, golden sand. The air smelled of salt, and a cool breeze blew constantly from the water. When I walked barefoot along the edge of the sea, the cold waves washed over my feet and pulled the sand from under my toes. Far away, small fishing boats rose and fell on the water like toys. Standing there, I felt both peaceful and amazed.

The beach itself is full of life, colour and sound throughout the day. Hundreds of colourful umbrellas and deck chairs lined the sand, and families sat under them eating snacks and taking photographs. Children built sandcastles and screamed with joy when the waves knocked them over. Young men raced along the shore on horses and beach bikes, while vendors walked up and down selling green coconuts, roasted corn and shell necklaces. The constant roar of the waves mixed with laughter, music and the shouts of sellers. Although the beach was crowded, its cheerful noise made everyone feel like part of a festival.

The most memorable part of the visit was the sunset over the Bay of Bengal. In the late afternoon, the crowd slowly gathered facing the west, and the noise seemed to soften. The sun turned from bright yellow to deep orange and then to red, and its reflection spread across the waves like a path of fire. The sky changed colour every few minutes, from gold to pink to purple. When the sun finally disappeared into the sea, many people clapped, and a quiet, calm darkness spread over the beach. I stood silently for a long time, unwilling to leave.

In conclusion, my visit to Cox's Bazar was a rich experience for all the senses. The endless sea, the lively and colourful beach and the magnificent sunset together made the trip unforgettable. Cox's Bazar is not just a famous tourist spot but a place where nature reminds us how small and lucky we are.
--- note
The **hook** is a scene (the first sight of the sea), followed by a general statement, background and the thesis, exactly like the slides. The aspects move from **the scene** (sea) to **the life on it** (beach) to **a climax** (sunset). The essay uses the **past tense** because it describes one particular visit. Notice the sensory range: salt smell, cold waves, roar of the waves, colours of the sky.
:::

:::model Model essay 2 · An event | The Celebration of Pohela Boishakh
--- outline
- **Topic:** The Celebration of Pohela Boishakh
- **Thesis Statement:** The celebration of Pohela Boishakh can be described through its colourful processions, its traditional food and dress, and the joyful spirit of the people.
- **Supporting Points/Plan of Development:** i) Colourful processions ii) Traditional food and dress iii) The joyful spirit of the people
- **Topic sentence of the first supporting paragraph:** The day begins with music and colourful processions that fill the streets.
- **Topic sentence of the second supporting paragraph:** Traditional food and dress give the festival its special Bengali character.
- **Topic sentence of the third supporting paragraph:** Above all, Pohela Boishakh is marked by the joyful spirit that unites people of all ages and backgrounds.
- **Concluding sentence:** Pohela Boishakh is more than the first day of the year; it is a living celebration of Bengali identity.
--- answer Essay
Long before sunrise, the beat of drums and the sound of flutes can be heard across Dhaka. Pohela Boishakh, the first day of the Bengali year, falls on the fourteenth of April and is one of the most widely celebrated festivals in Bangladesh. People of every religion, age and class join in, because the festival belongs to the shared culture of all Bengalis. On this day, the whole country seems to wear new clothes and a new smile. The celebration of Pohela Boishakh can be described through its colourful processions, its traditional food and dress, and the joyful spirit of the people.

The day begins with music and colourful processions that fill the streets. In Dhaka, crowds gather early at Ramna Park, where singers welcome the new year with songs under the old banyan tree. Later, the Mangal Shobhajatra, a grand procession led by students of the Faculty of Fine Arts, moves through the streets. Huge masks of owls, tigers and fish, painted in bright red, yellow and blue, rise above the crowd, and people carry paper birds and flowers on sticks. Drums beat, flutes play, and thousands of people walk together, singing and dancing. Similar processions and fairs take place in towns and villages across the country.

Traditional food and dress give the festival its special Bengali character. Women wear white saris with red borders, decorate their hair with flowers and paint patterns on their hands, while men wear colourful panjabis. The most famous dish of the day is panta bhat, rice soaked overnight in water, served with fried hilsa, mashed potatoes, green chillies and onions. Sweet shops are crowded with customers buying roshogolla and doi. At village fairs, stalls sell clay toys, handmade fans, sugar candies and puffed rice. The smell of fried fish and sweets fills the air, and the sight of so many red and white clothes makes the streets look like a flower garden.

Above all, Pohela Boishakh is marked by the joyful spirit that unites people of all ages and backgrounds. Strangers greet each other with "Shubho Noboborsho", children ride on swings and merry-go-rounds at the fairs, and families visit relatives with sweets. Shopkeepers open a new account book, called the halkhata, and invite customers to share sweets, hoping for good business in the new year. For one day, the worries of ordinary life seem to disappear, and the whole nation shares the same happiness and hope.

In conclusion, Pohela Boishakh is a festival that delights the eyes, the ears and the heart. Its vibrant processions, its traditional food and clothes and the warm spirit of togetherness make it a day like no other. Pohela Boishakh is more than the first day of the year; it is a living celebration of Bengali identity.
--- note
A **typical event** is described in the **present tense**. Each body paragraph gathers details from a different angle (sight and sound of the procession, sight, smell and taste of food and dress, feelings and customs). Accurate cultural details (Mangal Shobhajatra, halkhata, panta-ilish) make the description specific instead of general.
:::

:::model Model essay 3 · A person | My Mother, the Person I Admire Most
--- outline
- **Topic:** My Mother, the Person I Admire Most
- **Thesis Statement:** My mother can be described through her gentle appearance, her strong and patient personality, and her selfless care for others.
- **Supporting Points/Plan of Development:** i) Gentle appearance ii) Strong and patient personality iii) Selfless care for others
- **Topic sentence of the first supporting paragraph:** My mother's appearance reflects the gentleness of her nature.
- **Topic sentence of the second supporting paragraph:** Behind her gentle appearance lies a strong and patient personality.
- **Topic sentence of the third supporting paragraph:** The quality I admire most in my mother is her selfless care for everyone around her.
- **Concluding sentence:** My mother is not famous, but to me she is the most remarkable person in the world.
--- answer Essay
Every morning, the first sound in our house is my mother's quiet footsteps in the kitchen. Most people admire famous leaders, scientists or sports stars, but the person I admire most has never appeared on television. She is my mother, a schoolteacher who has spent her life caring for her students and her family. Everything I value, from hard work to kindness, I first learned by watching her. My mother can be described through her gentle appearance, her strong and patient personality, and her selfless care for others.

My mother's appearance reflects the gentleness of her nature. She is of medium height, with a round face, warm brown eyes and long black hair that has started to turn grey at the temples. She usually wears simple cotton saris in soft colours and a pair of thin gold bangles that she has worn for as long as I can remember. Her voice is soft and calm, and she smiles easily, so even strangers feel comfortable talking to her. When she listens to someone, she looks directly at them and nods slowly, as if nothing else in the world matters at that moment.

Behind her gentle appearance lies a strong and patient personality. When my father lost his job a few years ago, she never showed panic in front of us. Instead, she took extra tuition classes in the evening, planned our expenses carefully and encouraged everyone to stay hopeful. She is also extremely patient. When I failed my first mathematics test in secondary school, she did not scold me. She sat beside me every evening for a month and solved problems with me until I understood them. Her calm strength taught me that difficulties can be faced without losing hope.

The quality I admire most in my mother is her selfless care for everyone around her. She wakes up before everyone else to prepare breakfast and goes to bed only after the whole house is quiet. When a neighbour is ill, she is the first to visit with food. At her school, she often stays after classes to help weak students without asking for any payment, and several former students still visit her to thank her. She rarely buys anything for herself, yet she never forgets what others need.

In conclusion, my mother's gentle appearance, her quiet strength and her endless care for others make her the person I admire most. She has shaped my character more than any book or teacher. My mother is not famous, but to me she is the most remarkable person in the world.
--- note
Describing a **person** moves from **outside to inside**: appearance, then personality, then actions. Each personality claim is proved with a **short example** (losing the job, the failed maths test), which keeps the description concrete. The present tense is used for her as she is now, with the past for the examples.
:::

## Examination strategy

- **Interpret the prompt.** A descriptive topic names a subject: *A Rainy Day*, *A Village Market*, *My Favourite Place*. Check whether it suggests a typical scene (present tense) or a specific visit (past tense).
- **Organise quickly.** Use the aspect sets above. Write the thesis with the slides' pattern (*can be described through…*), then the three topic sentences.
- **Choose details.** For each aspect, list four or five specific details using different senses before you write. Avoid repeating the same detail in two paragraphs.
- **Avoid common errors:** a story with no description, three aspects that overlap (*the sea*, *the waves*, *the water*), a conclusion that adds new details, and tense switching.
- **Time:** outline 5 minutes, introduction 5, each body paragraph 7 to 8, conclusion 3, checking 3.
- **Unfamiliar subject?** Use *appearance / sounds and atmosphere / people and activities* for a place, or *appearance / personality / actions* for a person. These fit almost any topic.
