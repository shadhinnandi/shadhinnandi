# Vocabulary and Word-to-Sentence Practice

150 useful words for university writing, in three levels of 50 and fifteen groups of ten. Each word shows its Bengali meaning. Press **Show Sentences** to reveal an example sentence for every word in the group, and press again to hide them.

## How to study a group

1. **Read the ten words and their meanings.** Say each word aloud.
2. **Write your own sentence** for each word before revealing the examples.
3. **Press Show Sentences** and compare. Check the grammar around the word: which preposition follows it, whether it is a noun or a verb, singular or plural.
4. **Read the usage notes.** They mark common confusions (*affect / effect*, *advice / advise*, *borrow / lend*).
5. **Revise the next day** by covering the meanings and recalling them.

## Making sentences in the exam

The final asks you to *make grammatically correct and meaningful sentences* with given words (4 × 0.5 marks). A sentence earns the mark only if it shows the meaning of the word.

| Weak sentence | Why it is weak | Strong sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Crucial is a word. | Shows nothing about the meaning | Clean water is crucial for good health. |
| He is obstacle. | Wrong grammar: missing article, wrong use | Poverty is a major obstacle to education. |
| I like flexible. | Adjective used without a noun | Online classes offer a flexible schedule. |

> **Formula:** subject + correct form of the word + a detail that proves the meaning (*for good health*, *to education*). Keep it to one clear sentence of 8 to 15 words.

## Practice

:::practice Practice 1 · Sentence construction | Make sentences
Make grammatically correct and meaningful sentences with the following words.

i. convenient
ii. hesitate
iii. vulnerable
iv. consequence
--- skill
The final's word-to-sentence task
--- answer
i. The new bus stop is convenient for students who live near the campus.

ii. If you do not understand the lesson, do not hesitate to ask your teacher.

iii. Children who play near busy roads are vulnerable to accidents.

iv. One consequence of heavy rain in Dhaka is severe waterlogging.

*(Any sentence is correct if it is grammatical and clearly shows the meaning of the word.)*
:::

:::practice Practice 2 · Error identification | Correct the use of the word
Each sentence uses the word incorrectly. Correct it.

i. My father gave me many advices.
ii. Smoking effects your health.
iii. She participated the seminar.
iv. Can I lend your pen?
--- skill
Using the usage notes: countability, word class and prepositions
--- answer
i. My father gave me **a lot of advice** (or *many pieces of advice*). *Advice is uncountable.*

ii. Smoking **affects** your health. *"Affect" is the verb.*

iii. She participated **in** the seminar.

iv. Can I **borrow** your pen? *You borrow from someone; they lend to you.*
:::

:::practice Practice 3 · Exam level | Use advanced words in context
Rewrite each sentence, replacing the words in bold with a word from the Advanced list.

i. The flood caused damage that had **never happened before**.
ii. Mobile phones are **found everywhere** today.
iii. Planting trees can **reduce** the effects of heat.
iv. He was **not willing** to accept the new rules.
--- skill
Choosing precise academic vocabulary
--- answer
i. The flood caused **unprecedented** damage.

ii. Mobile phones are **ubiquitous** today.

iii. Planting trees can **mitigate** the effects of heat.

iv. He was **reluctant** to accept the new rules.
:::
