## Reading: multiple choice

**Midterm 1A** (passage on employee motivation and productivity) [0.50 × 4 = 2]

ii. What is "intrinsic motivation"?
iii. According to the passage, why is a "humane" workplace important?
iv. What is the best way to get people to do their best work?

**Final 1A** (passage on sleep and teenagers) [3 × 0.5 = 1.5]

## What the pattern tells you

- **Every paper** has a passage with multiple choice and short answers (two factual, one opinion).
- **Topic choices are labelled with their type.** Learn the structure of every type, not specific topics.
- **The outline is always required** before the paragraph or essay, in a fixed format.
- **Editing appears in both exams**, with the categories given in a box.
- The descriptive essay is taught in the slides but did not appear in the Spring 2026 final; it may appear in other trimesters.
