# Application Writing

## Purpose

An application is a **formal written request** to a person in authority: the head of a department, a registrar, a principal or a manager. At university, students write applications to ask for leave, permission, consideration of a problem, a fee waiver, a change of section and similar requests. In the final, it is one of two options for question 2D (3 marks).

An application must be **formal, polite, brief and clear**: the reader should understand who you are and what you want within a few seconds.

I, therefore, request you to kindly consider my problem and allow me to

## The format, part by part

| Order | Part | What to write | From the sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Date** | Month day, year, at the top left | *October 08, 2026* |
| 2 | **Recipient** | Title, department, institution, city, each on its own line | *The Head of Department / Department of Computer Science and Engineering / United International University / Dhaka, Bangladesh* |
| 3 | **Subject** | *Subject: Application for…* in one line stating the request | *Subject: Application for consideration of shortage of attendance due to illness* |
| 4 | **Salutation** | *Dear Sir,* or *Dear Madam,* | *Dear Sir,* |
| 5 | **Body paragraph 1** | Who you are, then the problem or situation and its result | *I am a student of… Unfortunately, I could not… As a result,…* |
| 6 | **Body paragraph 2** | The request, then thanks | *I, therefore, request you to kindly… I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.* |
| 7 | **Closing** | *Yours sincerely,* | *Yours sincerely,* |
| 8 | **Signature block** | Name, ID, department, university, each on its own line | *Name / ID / Department of CSE / United International University* |

### The key phrases

These phrases from the sample form the backbone of almost every application:

- *I am a student of the Department of…*
- *Unfortunately, …* (introduces the problem)
- *As a result, …* (shows the consequence)
- *I, therefore, request you to kindly …* (the request)
- *I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.* (the closing line of the body)

## Formal language and tone

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| I want leave. | I request you to kindly grant me leave… |
| Please give me permission fast. | I would be grateful if you could kindly allow me… |
| I was sick so I couldn't come. | Unfortunately, I could not attend classes due to illness. |
| Thanks a lot! | I shall be grateful for your kind consideration. |
| Contractions (*can't, I'm*) | Full forms (*cannot, I am*) |

## Common mistakes

- Putting the recipient before the date, or leaving out the date. The sample puts the **date first**.
- A vague subject such as *Subject: Application* or *Subject: Request*. Say exactly what you are applying for.
- Telling a long story. Two short paragraphs are enough: situation, then request.
- Forgetting the ID and department in the signature block.
- *Your sincerely* (incorrect) instead of *Yours sincerely*.
- Informal words, emotional language or demands.

## How to adapt the format to a new prompt

1. **Identify the recipient** from the question (*Head of the Department*, *Registrar*, *Librarian*). Write their title and institution in the recipient block.
2. **Turn the task into a subject line:** *"requesting permission to…"* → *Subject: Application for permission to…*
3. **Paragraph 1:** introduce yourself and explain the situation in two or three sentences, with one specific detail (dates, event name).
4. **Paragraph 2:** make the request with *I, therefore, request you to kindly…*, then close with *I shall be grateful…*
5. **Sign off** with the full signature block.

Use placeholders such as *[Your name]* and *[Your ID]* when practising, and your real details in the exam.

## Model applications

:::letter Model application 1 · Leave of absence | Application for Leave for a Family Emergency
**Prompt:** Write an application to the Head of the Department requesting three days' leave because of a family emergency.
--- outline
- **Recipient:** The Head of Department, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering
- **Subject:** leave of absence for three days
- **Paragraph 1:** introduce yourself; grandfather seriously ill in the village; must travel; dates of absence
- **Paragraph 2:** request leave for the three days and permission to collect missed materials; thanks
--- answer Application
November 10, 2026

The Head of Department
Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering
United International University
Dhaka, Bangladesh

Subject: Application for leave of absence for three days due to a family emergency

Dear Sir,
I am a student of the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. Unfortunately, my grandfather has become seriously ill in our village home in Rangpur, and my family has asked me to come home immediately. As a result, I will not be able to attend my classes from November 11 to November 13, 2026.

I, therefore, request you to kindly grant me leave of absence for these three days and allow me to collect the materials of the missed classes from my course teachers. I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]
ID: [Your ID]
Department of EEE
United International University
--- note
The subject line states the request **and** the reason. The first paragraph gives the exact **dates**, so the reader knows the scope of the request. The second paragraph adds a small, sensible extra request (collecting materials), which shows responsibility.
:::

:::letter Model application 2 · Financial request | Application for a Tuition Fee Waiver
**Prompt:** Write an application to the Registrar requesting a partial tuition fee waiver because of your family's financial difficulty.
--- outline
- **Recipient:** The Registrar, United International University
- **Subject:** partial tuition fee waiver due to financial hardship
- **Paragraph 1:** introduce yourself with department and result; father retired / income reduced; difficulty paying fees
- **Paragraph 2:** request a partial waiver for the coming trimester; mention you will submit documents; thanks
--- answer Application
December 02, 2026

The Registrar
United International University
United City, Madani Avenue
Dhaka, Bangladesh

Subject: Application for a partial tuition fee waiver due to financial hardship

Dear Sir,
I am a second-year student of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, and I have maintained a CGPA of 3.65 so far. Unfortunately, my father, who was the only earning member of our family, retired from his job this year, and our family income has fallen sharply. As a result, it has become very difficult for my family to pay my tuition fees for the coming trimester.

I, therefore, request you to kindly consider my situation and grant me a partial tuition fee waiver so that I can continue my studies without interruption. I am ready to submit any documents required to support my application. I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]
ID: [Your ID]
Department of CSE
United International University
--- note
A request involving money must be **factual and modest**. The applicant gives **evidence of merit** (CGPA) and of **need** (the father's retirement) in one paragraph, and offers documents. The tone stays formal: there is no emotional pleading.
:::

:::letter Model application 3 · Permission | Application for Permission to Use the Computer Lab after Class Hours
**Prompt:** Write an application to the Head of the Department requesting permission for your project team to use the computer lab after class hours.
--- outline
- **Recipient:** The Head of Department, Department of Computer Science and Engineering
- **Subject:** permission to use the computer lab after class hours
- **Paragraph 1:** introduce yourself as team leader; final-year project needs powerful computers; lab busy during the day
- **Paragraph 2:** request access from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. for two weeks; promise to follow lab rules; thanks
--- answer Application
October 20, 2026

The Head of Department
Department of Computer Science and Engineering
United International University
Dhaka, Bangladesh

Subject: Application for permission to use the computer lab after class hours

Dear Madam,
I am a student of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering and the leader of a four-member team working on our final-year project on image recognition. Our project requires computers with high processing power, which are available only in the department's computer lab. Unfortunately, the lab is fully booked for classes during the day. As a result, we are unable to run our experiments in time for the project deadline.

I, therefore, request you to kindly allow our team to use the computer lab from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays for the next two weeks. We promise to follow all lab rules and to take full responsibility for the equipment. I shall be grateful for your kind consideration.

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]
ID: [Your ID]
Department of CSE
United International University
--- note
A permission request should state **exactly what, when and for how long** (*5 p.m. to 8 p.m., weekdays, two weeks*). Offering a **promise** (following the lab rules) answers the reader's likely concern before it is asked. *Dear Madam* is used here to show the alternative salutation.
:::

## Examination strategy

- **Read the prompt for three things:** the recipient, the request and the reason. Each one goes into a fixed place in the format.
- **Start with the frame:** write the date, recipient block, subject line, salutation, closing and signature block first. That is most of the format mark.
- **Keep the body to two short paragraphs** (about 80 to 120 words in total).
- **Time:** about 10 to 12 minutes. Do not spend essay time on it.

## Practice

:::practice Practice 1 · Format | Put the parts in order
Arrange these parts of an application in the correct order, following the sample.

a) Yours sincerely, b) Subject: Application for… c) The date d) Dear Sir, e) The recipient's title and address f) Your name, ID and department g) The body paragraphs
--- skill
The order of the sample format
--- answer
**c → e → b → d → g → a → f.** Date, recipient, subject, salutation, body, closing, signature block.
