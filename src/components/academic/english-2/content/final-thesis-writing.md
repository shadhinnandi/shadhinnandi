# Thesis Statement Writing

## What a thesis statement is

A thesis statement is the **one sentence that states the main idea of the whole essay**. It is usually the last sentence of the introduction, and every paragraph of the essay exists to support it.

In the slides, the thesis does two jobs at once:

1. It states the **main point** (the controlling idea of the essay).
2. It lists the **plan of development**: the three supporting points that the body paragraphs will develop, in order.

> *Natural disasters are caused mainly by natural geological and climatic processes, as well as human activities that can intensify their occurrence and severity.*
>
> Main point: natural disasters have several causes. Plan: geological processes · climatic processes · human activities.

A thesis is to an essay what a topic sentence is to a paragraph. The difference is size: a topic sentence controls one paragraph; a thesis controls five.

## Thesis, topic, fact or opinion?

| Kind of sentence | Example | Is it a thesis? |
|---|---|---|
| **Topic** | Online classes. | No. It names a subject but makes no point. |
| **Fact** | Online classes use video-conferencing software. | No. Nothing to argue or develop. |
| **Vague opinion** | Online classes are good. | No. Good in what way? There is no plan. |
| **Announcement** | In this essay, I will discuss online classes. | No. It says what you will do, not what you think. |
| **Thesis** | Online classes are convenient because they save travel time, offer recorded lectures and allow students to study from anywhere. | **Yes.** A clear point plus a plan of development. |

## The thesis formula from the slides

Every thesis in the final slides follows this pattern:

> **Topic + main point + (because / through / in terms of) + point 1, point 2, and point 3.**

| Essay type | Thesis from the slides |
|---|---|
| Descriptive | A rainy day can be described **through** its beautiful surroundings, soothing sounds, and the different activities of people during the rain. |
| Cause and effect | Natural disasters are caused by geological and climatic processes as well as human activities, **and they result in** serious consequences for human life, the economy, and the environment. |
| Argumentative | I strongly believe that attendance should be mandatory for university students **because** regular attendance improves learning, develops responsibility, and encourages active participation in class. |
| Compare and contrast | Although online and offline classes share several similarities, they differ significantly **in terms of** interaction, flexibility, and learning environment. |

## Parallelism: the three points must match

The three points in a thesis must have the **same grammatical form**. This is called parallel structure, and it is exactly what question 2B tests: you must add a third point that matches the first two.

| Not parallel | Parallel |
|---|---|
| …because it keeps you active, saves money, and **the environment is cleaner**. | …because it keeps you active, saves money, and **reduces pollution**. |
| …through its **beautiful surroundings**, **soothing sounds**, and **people are busy**. | …through its beautiful surroundings, soothing sounds, and **the activities of people**. |
| …improves learning, develops responsibility, and **to participate**. | …improves learning, develops responsibility, and **encourages participation**. |

**How to check:** look at the first word of each point. If the first two start with verbs in the same form (*keeps*, *saves*), the third must too (*reduces*). If they are noun phrases (*beautiful surroundings*, *soothing sounds*), the third must be a noun phrase.

## Completing a thesis statement (question 2B)

The task gives a thesis with two points and a blank. To add the third point:

1. **Find the main point.** What is the thesis arguing? (*Walking to school is a great choice.*)
2. **Read the first two points** and identify their grammatical form (verb phrases? noun phrases?).
3. **Think of a third point that is different** from the first two (not a repeat in other words) and that supports the same main point.
4. **Write it in the same form**, and keep it about the same length.
5. **Read the whole sentence aloud** to check that it flows.

### Worked example

*Reading books regularly is a valuable habit because it improves vocabulary, strengthens concentration, and ________.*

- Main point: reading regularly is valuable.
- Form: verb + object (*improves vocabulary*, *strengthens concentration*).
- New, different benefit: knowledge, imagination, stress relief.
- Answer: *…and **broadens our knowledge of the world**.* ✓
- Wrong: *…and books are interesting.* ✗ (not parallel) · *…and helps us learn words.* ✗ (repeats point 1)

## Writing your own thesis

1. **Choose your main point.** For an argumentative essay, choose your side. For other types, decide the overall idea.
2. **Brainstorm** five or six supporting ideas.
3. **Select three** that are distinct, can each fill a paragraph, and do not overlap.
4. **Order them** logically (time, importance, natural grouping).
5. **Combine** with the slides' formula, in parallel form.
6. **Test it** with the editing checklist in the **Thesis Statement Editing** lesson: not an announcement, not too broad, not too narrow.

## Checking alignment with the body

After writing the outline, check:

- Each thesis point = one plan-of-development item = one body paragraph topic sentence.
- The order is the same in all three places.
- The topic sentences repeat the key word of each point (*surroundings*, *sounds*, *activities*).

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Announcing | This essay is about pollution. | Air pollution harms health, damages crops and raises medical costs. |
| No plan | Pollution is a serious problem. | Add the three points. |
| Points overlap | …saves money, saves cash and is cheap. | Choose three different ideas. |
| Not parallel | …saves money, keeps you fit, and the air is clean. | …saves money, keeps you fit, and cleans the air. |
| A question | Is pollution dangerous? | A thesis is a statement. |
| Two sentences | Pollution is bad. It harms people. | One clear sentence. |

## Revision summary

> **Thesis = topic + main point + three parallel points.** One sentence, last in the introduction.
> Not a topic, fact, vague opinion or announcement. The three points become the plan of development and the three topic sentences, in the same order.

## Practice

:::practice Practice 1 · Basic | Thesis or not?
Which of these is a thesis statement? For the others, say what they are (topic, fact, vague opinion or announcement).

i. The metro rail in Dhaka.
ii. The metro rail has reduced travel time, cut fuel costs and made commuting more comfortable for Dhaka residents.
iii. I am going to write about the metro rail.
iv. The metro rail is nice.
v. The first section of the Dhaka metro rail opened in 2022.
--- skill
Distinguishing a thesis from a topic, fact, opinion or announcement
--- answer
i. **Topic** (no point).
ii. **Thesis** (main point plus three parallel points).
iii. **Announcement** (states the writer's plan, not a point).
iv. **Vague opinion** ("nice" in what way? no plan).
v. **Fact** (nothing to develop).
:::

:::practice Practice 2 · Guided | Complete the thesis statements
Add a third supporting point in parallel form.

i. Learning a second language is beneficial because it improves job prospects, helps people understand other cultures, and ________.
ii. A university library is an ideal place to study because of its quiet atmosphere, its wide collection of books, and ________.
iii. Planting trees in cities is important because trees clean the air, provide shade, and ________.
--- skill
Question 2B: parallel third points
--- hint
(i) uses verb phrases (*improves…, helps…*); (ii) uses noun phrases after *because of* (*its quiet atmosphere, its wide collection*); (iii) uses verbs with the subject *trees*.
--- answer
i. …and **strengthens memory and thinking skills**.

ii. …and **its free access to the internet**.

iii. …and **provide homes for birds and insects**. *(or: reduce flooding by absorbing rainwater)*

*(Any third point is correct if it is different from the first two, supports the main point and matches their grammatical form.)*
:::

:::practice Practice 3 · Error identification | Fix the parallelism
Rewrite each thesis so the three points are parallel.

i. Regular exercise is important because it strengthens the heart, reduces stress, and for better sleep.
ii. A good teacher is patient, knowledgeable, and explains clearly.
--- skill
Correcting faulty parallelism in a thesis
--- answer
i. Regular exercise is important because it strengthens the heart, reduces stress, and **improves sleep**.

ii. A good teacher is patient, knowledgeable, and **clear in explanation**. *(or: A good teacher shows patience, has deep knowledge and explains clearly.)*
:::

:::practice Practice 4 · Exam level | Write thesis statements for each essay type
Write a thesis statement with three supporting points for each topic.

i. A Festival in Bangladesh *(Descriptive)*
ii. Causes of Road Accidents *(Cause and Effect)*
iii. Should Mobile Phones Be Allowed in Classrooms? *(Argumentative)*
iv. Living in a City vs. Living in a Village *(Compare/Contrast)*
--- skill
Writing a thesis that fits each essay type
--- answer
i. Pohela Boishakh can be described through its colourful processions, its traditional food and the joyful spirit of the people who celebrate it.

ii. Road accidents in Bangladesh are caused mainly by reckless driving, poorly maintained vehicles and weak enforcement of traffic laws.

iii. I strongly believe that mobile phones should not be allowed in classrooms because they distract students, encourage cheating and reduce face-to-face interaction.

iv. Although city life and village life both offer a sense of community, they differ significantly in terms of opportunities, cost of living and environment.
:::
