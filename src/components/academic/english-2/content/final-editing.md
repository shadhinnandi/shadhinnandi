# Editing: Eight Errors

## What changes in the final

The final's editing question works exactly like the midterm's, with two differences:

| | Midterm | Final |
|---|---|---|
| Number of errors | 4 (0.5 each = 2 marks) | **8** (0.5 each = **4 marks**) |
| New category | none | **Faulty parallelism** |

The Spring 2026 final's box listed eight categories: **fragment, run-on, irregular verb form, inconsistent verb tense, faulty parallelism, subject-verb agreement, mistake in apostrophe, missing comma**. With eight errors and eight categories, each category usually appears once, which helps you check your answers.

All the error types are explained with examples in **Editing and Proofreading** in the midterm section. This lesson adds faulty parallelism and a method for handling eight errors quickly.

## Faulty parallelism

**Parallelism** means that items joined in a list or a pair have the **same grammatical form**. Faulty parallelism breaks the pattern.

| Wrong | Correct | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| The trip included visiting a museum, exploring a park, and to eat lunch. | …visiting a museum, exploring a park, and **eating** lunch. | -ing, -ing, -ing |
| She likes to read, to write, and painting. | She likes to read, to write, and **to paint**. | to + verb ×3 |
| The job requires patience, honesty, and being careful. | …patience, honesty, and **carefulness** (or *care*). | noun ×3 |
| He studied hard, revised his notes, and is confident. | He studied hard, revised his notes, and **felt** confident. | past verbs ×3 |
| The room was clean, bright, and had a lot of space. | The room was clean, bright, and **spacious**. | adjective ×3 |

**How to identify:** look for **lists of three** and pairs joined by *and, or, but, both… and, either… or, not only… but also*. Check the first word of each item; they must match.

You have already met parallelism in **Thesis Statement Writing**: the three points of a thesis must be parallel. The editing question tests the same rule.

## A method for eight errors

1. **Read the paragraph once** for meaning and **time**: is the story in the past or the present?
2. **Go through the numbers in order.** For each one, read the whole sentence, not just the numbered word.
3. **Run the checklist:**
   - Is it a complete sentence? → *fragment*
   - Are two sentences joined without punctuation? → *run-on*
   - Does the verb form exist? (*sayed, goed*) → *irregular verb form*
   - Is the verb in the wrong time? (*see* in a past story) → *inconsistent tense*
   - Does the verb match its subject? (*the cost… were*) → *subject-verb agreement*
   - Is it the third item in a list? → *faulty parallelism*
   - Is there a contraction or possession? (*dont, students books*) → *apostrophe*
   - Is there an introductory phrase or a list? → *missing comma*
4. **Write each answer line:** number, category letter and name, correction.
5. **Tick off categories** in the box. If two answers share a category and another category is unused, check those two again.

## Worked example

The paragraph below was written for this lesson in the final's format. Each numbered word contains one mistake.

> The volunteers ¹was ready early on Saturday morning. They planned to clean the riverbank, plant trees, and ²collecting plastic waste. ³When the bus arrived they quickly loaded their tools. The work was hard. ⁴Because the sun was very strong. By noon, the team had ⁵gathered forty bags of rubbish everyone was exhausted. Their leader ⁶bringed cold water for everyone. Later, the ⁷villagers smiles showed how grateful they were. At the end of the day, everyone ⁸says it was worth the effort.

| No. | Category | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subject-verb agreement | The volunteers **were** ready |
| 2 | Faulty parallelism | clean the riverbank, plant trees, and **collect** plastic waste |
| 3 | Missing comma | When the bus arrived**,** they quickly loaded their tools. |
| 4 | Fragment | The work was hard **because** the sun was very strong. |
| 5 | Run-on | gathered forty bags of rubbish**, and** everyone was exhausted. *(or: …rubbish. Everyone was exhausted.)* |
| 6 | Irregular verb form | Their leader **brought** cold water |
| 7 | Mistake in apostrophe | the **villagers'** smiles |
| 8 | Inconsistent verb tense | everyone **said** it was worth the effort |

## Common traps

- **Subject-verb agreement with "of" phrases:** *The cost of tickets and lunch **was** affordable.* The subject is *cost*.
- **Fragment versus run-on:** a fragment is too little (one clause that cannot stand alone); a run-on is too much (two sentences squeezed together).
- **Irregular form versus inconsistent tense:** *sayed* is a form that does not exist (irregular verb form); *says* is a real form in the wrong tense (inconsistent tense).
- **Plural possession:** *students' books*, *villagers' smiles* (apostrophe after the s). Irregular plurals: *children's*, *people's*.
- **Contractions:** *don't*, *didn't*, *it's* need apostrophes; *its* (possession) does not.

## Revision summary

> Eight numbered errors, usually one of each category in the box. Check the time of the passage first. Answer line: *number · category · correction*. New in the final: **faulty parallelism**, so make every item in a list match.

## Practice

:::practice Practice 1 · Basic | Fix the parallelism
Correct the faulty parallelism.

i. My brother enjoys swimming, cycling, and to play cricket.
ii. The teacher asked us to read the chapter, answer the questions, and that we should submit them on Monday.
iii. The hotel was cheap, clean, and had a friendly staff.
--- skill
Recognising and correcting faulty parallelism
--- answer
i. My brother enjoys swimming, cycling, and **playing** cricket.

ii. The teacher asked us to read the chapter, answer the questions, and **submit** them on Monday.

iii. The hotel was cheap, clean, and **friendly**. *(or: The hotel was cheap and clean and had a friendly staff.)*
:::

:::practice Practice 2 · Exam format | Eight mistakes
The following paragraph contains eight mistakes among these categories: **a.** fragment **b.** run-on **c.** irregular verb form **d.** inconsistent verb tense **e.** faulty parallelism **f.** subject verb agreement **g.** mistake in apostrophe **h.** missing comma. Identify, write and correct them.

Last month, our department ¹organise a science fair. Each of the teams ²were asked to build a working model. ³After weeks of preparation the students displayed their projects in the main hall. One group had built a solar water pump, another had designed a smart dustbin, and a third ⁴creating a weather station. ⁵Which surprised the judges. The hall was crowded ⁶visitors kept arriving all afternoon. The ⁷judges decision was announced at five o'clock. Our team ⁸winned second prize, and we celebrated with sweets.
--- skill
The final's 4-mark editing task
--- hint
The passage is in the past (*Last month*). Look at the subject of sentence 2 (*Each*). Check the list in sentence 4.
--- answer
1. **d. inconsistent verb tense:** our department **organised** a science fair.
2. **f. subject verb agreement:** Each of the teams **was** asked to build a working model.
3. **h. missing comma:** After weeks of preparation**,** the students displayed their projects.
4. **e. faulty parallelism:** …and a third **had created** a weather station.
5. **a. fragment:** …a weather station**, which** surprised the judges.
6. **b. run-on:** The hall was crowded**, and** visitors kept arriving all afternoon. *(or: crowded. Visitors kept arriving…)*
7. **g. mistake in apostrophe:** The **judges'** decision was announced.
8. **c. irregular verb form:** Our team **won** second prize.
:::
