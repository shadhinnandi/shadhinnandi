# Midterm Introduction

The midterm checks two things: whether you can read a passage carefully, and whether you can write one well-organised paragraph. It also checks whether you can spot and correct small grammar mistakes, because a clear paragraph with careless errors still loses marks.

## What the midterm covers

| Part | Marks | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Reading** | 12 | Read a passage of about 600 words, then answer multiple-choice questions, match words to definitions, write short answers in your own words and write a summary |
| **B. Writing** | 8 | Find and correct four mistakes in a short paragraph, then write one paragraph of 150 to 200 words with its outline |
| Listening and speaking | 10 | Tested separately in class |

## The skills behind the marks

- **Reading strategies.** Skimming finds the main idea, scanning finds a fact, and inferencing reads between the lines. Each multiple-choice question uses one of them.
- **Answering in your own words.** Short answers and the summary lose marks if you copy sentences from the passage. Paraphrasing is a skill you practise, not a talent.
- **Paragraph structure.** Every paragraph has a topic sentence, supporting sentences and a concluding sentence. The exam asks you to write the outline first, so the structure itself earns marks.
- **Paragraph types.** The writing question offers a choice of topics, each labelled with a type: descriptive, narrative, process, or classification. The slides also teach the cause-and-effect paragraph. Each type has its own way of ordering ideas.
- **Editing.** The editing question names the error categories in a box. You must find each error, name its category and write the correction.

## How the topics connect

Reading and writing support each other. When you summarise a passage, you look for its main idea and supporting points, exactly the parts you must build when you write a paragraph. When you edit someone else's paragraph, you learn to check your own. Study the lessons in order: each one uses skills from the one before.

## How to use the midterm lessons

1. Open **Midterm Course Structure** for a one-page view of every topic.
2. Work through each lesson in order. Read the explanation and the step-by-step method, study the model answers and read the exam strategy.
3. Do the practice at the end of each lesson on paper before revealing the answers.
4. When you finish, take the real Spring 2026 midterm paper under exam time (1 hour 30 minutes, or about 60 minutes for the reading and writing parts) and compare your answers with the full solutions.

> **Time plan for the exam:** about 30 minutes for Part A (reading), 5 minutes for the editing question and 20 to 25 minutes for the paragraph. Leave 5 minutes to check your writing for the same errors the editing question tests.
