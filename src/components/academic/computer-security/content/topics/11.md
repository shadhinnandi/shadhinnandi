# DDoS and Botnets

## DoS and DDoS

- **DoS (Denial of Service):** an attack that makes a server or network unavailable to its real users. It attacks **availability**.
- **DDoS (Distributed DoS):** the same, but the attack traffic comes from **many computers at once**, usually a botnet. It is much stronger and much harder to block, because there is no single source to filter.

**A DDoS attack can target a server in three ways:**

1. **Network bandwidth:** fill the victim's link with too much traffic.
2. **Computing resources:** use up the server's CPU and memory.
3. **Server vulnerabilities:** use a software bug (such as a buffer overflow or logic bug) to crash the operating system or a program such as Apache, Sendmail or SQL server.

## Formats of DoS/DDoS attacks

| Format | Idea | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Real IP-based attack using botnets** | Bots send traffic from their real IPs; the attacker does not care if bots are exposed. | TCP flood, UDP flood, ICMP flood |
| **Spoofed IP-based attack** | Fake source IPs hide where the traffic comes from. | SYN flood with spoofed IPs |
| **Source address hiding (reflection)** | Innocent third parties reply to the victim, hiding the true source. | Smurf attack |

## What is a botnet?

A **botnet** is a network of compromised computers (**bots** or **zombies**) controlled remotely by an attacker (the **botmaster**) through **Command and Control (C&C)** servers.

- Bots are infected the same way as worms and viruses spread, then quietly wait for commands.
- The owners usually do not know their computers are part of a botnet.

**In one line:** the botmaster sends one command to the C&C server, and thousands of bots act on it together.

**Attacks launched with botnets:**

- DDoS attacks
- Spam and phishing email forwarding
- Click fraud on online ads
- Keystroke logging and theft of passwords and bank details
- Spreading more malware

Botnets are a top concern for banks and online shops because of fraud and DDoS.

## Monitoring botnets: honeypots

A **honeypot** is a deliberately vulnerable computer set up by researchers to **attract malware**. When it gets infected and joins a botnet, researchers can watch the botnet from inside ("honeypot spy").

- **Passive monitoring:** log all network traffic to see the bot's communication and the commands it receives.
- **Active monitoring:** actively contact other bots to learn more, such as neighbour lists and other C&C servers.

## Countermeasures

**Against botnets:**

- Keep systems patched and run anti-virus, so computers do not become bots.
- Use firewalls and intrusion detection to spot C&C traffic and unusual outgoing traffic.
- Find and take down C&C servers (often with the help of ISPs and law enforcement).
- Use honeypots to study botnets and their commands.

**Against DDoS:**

- Rate limiting and traffic filtering at routers and firewalls.
- Ingress/egress filtering to drop spoofed source IPs.
- Extra capacity, load balancing and content delivery networks (CDNs) to absorb traffic.
- DDoS protection services that clean traffic before it reaches the server.
- An incident response plan with the ISP to block attack traffic upstream.

## Exam-ready answers

**Q. What is a botnet? How is it used for a DDoS attack? (4 marks)**

A botnet is a group of compromised computers (bots or zombies) controlled by an attacker through Command and Control servers.

1. The attacker infects many computers with bot malware.
2. The bots connect to the C&C server and wait.
3. The botmaster sends one command naming the victim.
4. All bots send traffic to the victim at the same time, overloading its bandwidth or resources so real users cannot be served.

Since traffic comes from thousands of real machines in many places, it is very hard to filter.

**Q. Write the countermeasures against botnets. (3 marks)**

- Patch systems and use anti-virus to prevent infection.
- Monitor for unusual outgoing and C&C traffic with firewalls and IDS.
- Take down C&C servers, and use honeypots to study botnets.
- Use rate limiting, filtering and DDoS protection to reduce attack impact.
